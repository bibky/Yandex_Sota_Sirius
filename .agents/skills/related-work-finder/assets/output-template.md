### Evidence and score rationales

For each selected paper give four short rationales (or the missing evidence for N/A), a source locator, material reviewed and confidence rationale. Distinguish source findings from your interpretation. Specify versions and avoid asserting citation relationships without verification.

## 3. Related Work Analysis

Use topic-specific groups. Compare approaches within and across groups; distinguish predecessors, contemporaries and later work. Support concrete claims with adjacent bibliographic links. Do not infer priority or missing methods from silence in an abstract.

## 4. Key Findings

State closest methods, complementary approaches, supported distinctions and limits of the comparison. Do not compare runtime/accuracy numerically across incompatible experiments.

## 5. References and Limitations

Give verified authors, titles, dates, source types and DOI/URLs for selected papers. Record status for the version reviewed and the source, date and stated reason of any retraction/withdrawal notice. Exclude confirmed retracted/withdrawn versions from the final selection, retain candidate decisions, and check other versions independently. Disclose unverified status. Explain unavailable texts, partial readings, incomplete applicable routes and periods, and unmet count constraints. State whether the report is exploratory.
